Intro (English)

Morui is a WhatsApp-based livestock health assistant built for farmers across Botswana. It supports cattle 🐄, goats 🐐, chickens 🐓, dogs 🐕, and horses 🐎—offering symptom checks, vet referrals, and practical advice in both Setswana and English.

Intro (Setswana)

Morui ke mothusi wa tlhokomelo ya diphologolo ka WhatsApp, o agilweng go thusa barui mo Botswana. O thusa ka dikgomo 🐄, dipodi 🐐, dikoko 🐓, dintša 🐕, le dipitse 🐎—ka go sekaseka matshwao, go kopa thuso ya ngaka, le go fa kgakololo e e dirisang.

Mission (English)

Our goal is simple: empower farmers with instant, reliable support—even in remote areas.
